# Summary

Part A has 135 past-paper questions (PYQ-1 to PYQ-135), all from papers set by Prof. P. Krishna Reddy. Part B has 174 predicted questions (PRED-1 to PRED-174). Both are sorted into the requested units and topics.

## 1. Papers used

All papers below were set by Prof. P. Krishna Reddy (the 2020, 2021 and 2022 OSN papers carry his style and his answer keys, and his 2025 AOS Quiz 1 reused the 2022 OSN Quiz 1 paper). His style: short explain / justify / compare questions, TRUE/FALSE correct-and-justify, and "motivation for the transition" questions.

- OSN (CS3.301) Quizzes 1 to 9, 2020. Question papers, no key (OSN_papers_2020_M.pdf). Quiz 3 has two sets. Marks for Quizzes 1 to 3 are not printed per question; they are given as the paper total divided equally (4 marks).
- OSN 2020 quiz solution sheets, alternate sets of Quizzes 2, 4, 6, 8 and 9: one student's handwritten answers from a senior's repository (claude/OSN_extra_PYQs_senior_repo.md). The student copied only the opening words of many questions, and no marks are recorded.
- OSN End-sem 2021, question list: a TA allocation sheet in the same repository; every statement is cut off and no marks are given.
- OSN Quiz 1, 2022, with the professor's key: ten 2-mark questions.
- OSN Quiz 2, 2022, with the professor's key (scanned; OCR): ten 2-mark questions.
- OSN Mid-sem 2022, with the professor's key (scanned; partly illegible).
- OSN End-sem 2022, question cum answer booklet with the key (scanned; OCR). 80 marks: Q1 ten 1-mark TRUE/FALSE, Q2 five 3-mark questions, Q3 to Q13 5 marks each.
- AOS (CS3.304) Quiz 1, 2025: the same paper and key as OSN Quiz 1 2022, so each of its questions counts once for 2022 and once for 2025.
- AOS Mid-sem 2025 (23 Sept 2025, 45 marks), including the professor's written answer to Q6 (progressive round robin).
- AOS Quiz 2, 2025 (16 marks, eight 2-mark questions). No key.
- AOS End-sem 2025 (25 Nov 2025, 80 marks). No key. Photographed pages, transcribed in claude/AOS_2025_papers_transcribed.md.
- AOS Quiz 2, 2026 (this year, ten 2-mark questions), with answers (Quiz2_OSN_Questions_and_Answers.pdf).

**Papers excluded on purpose:** OSN End-sem 2023 ("IndixOS"), OSN Mid-sem 2025 ("LangOS") and OSN End-sem 2025 ("AgentOS"). They are scenario-style papers with no key and no printed examiner, in a style unlike Prof. Reddy's, so they are taken to be set by someone else. Their 37 questions are left out, and their occurrences are removed from questions that were also asked elsewhere (for example the TCP handshake MCQ).

**Questions missing or incomplete because a scan or source was incomplete:**

- OSN End-sem 2022: the pages for Q1.4, Q2.4, Q12 and Q13.1 are missing from the scan, so these four questions cannot be listed. The Q6 page is also incomplete; its question is known from repeats (End 2021 Q11, AOS End 2025 Q9).
- OSN Mid-sem 2022: the page with Q7 (the single-lane bridge) is missing; only the key's monitor code survives. Other questions on the scan are partly illegible.
- OSN End-sem 2022 Q10 (three-way handshake) is only partly legible.
- OSN End-sem 2021 list: every statement is truncated. Where the notes completed one, the completion follows the textbook exercise it comes from.
- OSN 2020 alternate sets: only the opening words of most questions survive. For Quiz 4 (alt set) Q2, a TRUE/FALSE item, the statement itself is not recorded.
- Figures missing: OSN End-sem 2021 Q10 (the broadcast network) and AOS End-sem 2025 Q1.8 (the bridge/switch network).

## 3. Notes on the export

- Marks in "Asked in" are per paper. "not recorded" means the source is a student answer sheet; "not given" means the 2021 question list.
- Answers come from the notes built in this project. "professor's key" means the answer starts from the professor's key and may add points after it. "student answers" is used only where a student's answer sheet is the sole source. Everything else is written by Claude, with no key.
- Marking points were drawn from each answer's core sentence, its steps and its final value; check them against the answer before relying on them for grading.
- PYQ counts by unit: 1 Foundations: 22, 2 Processes: 47, 3 Networking: 29, 4 Memory: 17, 5 Storage and Distributed Systems: 18, Other: 2.
- Predicted counts by unit: 1 Foundations: 12, 2 Processes: 44, 3 Networking: 9, 4 Memory: 31, 5 Storage and Distributed Systems: 64, Other: 14.
